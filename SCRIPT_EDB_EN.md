## 6 — THE LEGAL FRAMEWORK (2:45 → 3:55)

Teleconsultation is not a legal debate. It has been an established fact for nearly thirty years.

Since 1997, the World Health Organization has defined it as a medical act in its own right. France has reimbursed it since 2018. The United States since the Medicare Act, extended to all fifty states. The United Kingdom, Germany, Australia, Canada and Switzerland have written it into their ordinary law.

And in Mauritius? The Medical Council Act of nineteen ninety-nine imposes no requirement of physical presence.

A medical act is defined by the quality of its author — a registered physician — and by its clinical nature. Not by the technical channel.

What is not forbidden is permitted.

A Tibok teleconsultation is therefore a legal, enforceable, adjudicable medical act — a clear framework Mauritius can claim, raise into a standard, and export.

## 9 — SILENTCHECK: ACTIVE PREVENTION (6:45 → 8:05)

Our flagship innovation is called SilentCheck.

It is a cardiovascular risk-stratification tool, based on the BSD Score — for Bach, Sampol, Dignat-George.

A science built on four million patients followed across fifty-two countries. Fifty-three first-rank scientific references, published in the New England Journal of Medicine, The Lancet, JACC, Circulation. Sixty-nine thousand six hundred and twelve individuals in the validation cohort. Eleven ethnic profiles with seven multipliers adapted to the Mauritian population.

Fifteen biomarkers analysed. Three check-up tiers depending on risk level.

SilentCheck detects, five to ten years in advance, the anomalies symptoms do not yet reveal.

Eighty percent of heart disease develops without symptoms for a decade. Eighty-five percent of complications are avoidable with early detection.

A traditional health system looks in the rear-view mirror: it measures and pays for what has already cost. SilentCheck looks ahead. It is the building block that moves Mauritius from medicine endured to active prevention. That is what prevention means: acting before the disease. Not after.

## 11 — RELEVANCE CONTROL (8:45 → 9:55)

And this is precisely where the module that changes the economics of healthcare sits.

Today, you verify a document: a form, a receipt, a prescription — reconstructed after the fact. Nobody observed the act. Fraud, over-prescription, the phantom act are detected late, on paper, partially.

Tibok reverses that model. Every prescription, every test order, every proposed intervention passes through the platform — natively provable: time-stamping, electronic signature, video traceability, and a relevance analysis generated by the Medical Intelligence.

We move from ex-post control on paper to native, real-time control — for insurers, employers, the State.

Three structural levers:

**An end to over-prescription** — every test, every act is confronted with the guidelines before it enters the expense. Opportunistic prescribing becomes visible.

**A structured fight against fraud** — time-stamping, electronic signature, video traceability. The phantom act and up-coding have no blind spot left.

**Command of healthcare costs** — early detection, coordinated care paths, structured clinical data to steer the expense.

And it is an export-ready product — for every market where healthcare is still steered on paper.
