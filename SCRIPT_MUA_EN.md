## 6 — THE LEGAL FRAMEWORK (2:45 → 3:55)

Teleconsultation is not a legal debate. It has been an established fact for nearly thirty years.

Since 1997, the World Health Organization has defined it as a medical act in its own right. France has reimbursed it since 2018. The United States since the Medicare Act, extended to all fifty states. The United Kingdom, Germany, Australia, Canada and Switzerland have written it into ordinary law.

And in Mauritius? The Medical Council Act of nineteen ninety-nine imposes no requirement of physical presence.

A medical act is defined by the standing of its author — a registered physician — and by its clinical nature. Not by the technical channel.

What is not forbidden is permitted.

A Tibok teleconsultation is therefore a legal, enforceable, adjudicable medical act — one that MUA can cover and reimburse under ordinary law, within the schedule of benefits it already runs.
